About aima

Last updated: 14 October 2026

aima is a small web app that shows a household's money summary from one Google Sheet. It runs at aima.davidentrena.com, in English, Spanish and Russian, and you can install it on your phone like any other app.

What aima shows

  • The Total: the money of the household, from its parts: the bank accounts, the credit cards, the money set aside, and the cash. You can switch each part on or off to see the Total without it.
  • The spending goal: how much the household has spent in a period, compared with a limit, and the spending of each month, movement by movement.

aima only shows these numbers. It never changes anything in the spreadsheet.

Who can use it

Only the people the owner of the spreadsheet shares it with. Access is decided by the spreadsheet's sharing settings in Google Sheets, not by aima. If the spreadsheet is not shared with you, Google refuses to give aima the data, and aima signs you out with a message.

What Google data aima uses, and why

When you sign in with Google, aima asks for two permissions:

  • "See your primary Google Account email address", so your next sign-in can be one tap, without choosing the account again.
  • "See all your Google Sheets spreadsheets", read-only, so aima can read the cells it shows from the one spreadsheet it is set up for. It reads nothing else, and it cannot change or delete anything.

aima has no server. The data goes straight from Google to your browser and is not kept anywhere else. The Privacy Policy explains all the details, and the Terms of Service explain the rules for using aima.

How to start

Open aima and tap "Login with Google". Use the Google account that the spreadsheet is shared with.
